Executive Delivery — Consulting Request to Firm-Wide Rollout

Unified Leadership Dashboard

One view of the day for every L1 leader — built from a consulting request, scaled by design

Internal Consulting API Integrations Executive Stakeholders Config-Driven Scaling Firm-Wide Rollout

How It Started

This one came to me as a consulting request from firm leadership. L1 leaders and their assistants were toggling across five disconnected platforms — Outlook, OneDrive, internal portals, external press feeds — just to piece together a single day's priorities. Nobody had asked for a product; they'd asked for help.

The Problem Underneath

Each platform did its own job fine. The cost was the assembly: every morning, someone senior (or their assistant) was manually stitching together meetings, documents, press coverage, and internal updates from five places. The information existed; the single view of it didn't.

What I Built

A unified leadership dashboard that pulls meetings, key documents, press releases, and internal updates into one view, via API connections across all five sources. The leader opens one page and sees their day.

Users & Stakeholders

  • L1 leaders
  • Executive assistants
  • Firm leadership (sponsors)

Sources Unified

  • Outlook (meetings)
  • OneDrive (key documents)
  • Internal portals
  • External press feeds
  • Internal updates

The Decision That Made It Scale

API Connections, Not Copies

Each source stays the system of record. The dashboard connects over APIs and assembles the view live — no duplicate data stores to govern, no sync jobs to babysit.

Config-Driven Onboarding

The part I pushed hardest for: a config-driven scaling pattern, which I proposed and got signed off. Adding a new leader is configuration, not a rebuild of five integrations. That decision is why the rollout could go firm-wide.

Adopted at the Top

The executive team adopted it first, then it rolled out across all L1 leaders firm-wide. Executive tools live or die on the first week's impression — this one stuck.

Key Takeaways

Two things I took from this project. First, consulting requests are product discovery in disguise — "can you help us with this?" from leadership was really a problem statement nobody had framed yet. Second, the scaling decision mattered more than the dashboard itself. A one-off build for the executive team would have been a nice demo; the config-driven pattern is what turned it into infrastructure the firm can keep extending without me in the loop. That's the difference between delivering a build and delivering a capability.
