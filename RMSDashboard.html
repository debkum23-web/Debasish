Giving business units a dashboard they trust instead of a spreadsheet nobody updates

SharePoint Governance Insight Dashboard — Acting Product Owner

Power BI SharePoint Online Data Modeling Governance Stakeholder Discovery

Context

Business units needed a straight answer to "how much SharePoint storage do we actually have, and who's responsible for it" — and couldn't get one without someone manually pulling it together first.

Problem

The details that answer that question — site collection URL, which record manager and business unit owns it, library name, library size, item counts — lived scattered across different sources. Every time someone needed the full picture, they were stitching it together by hand, and by the time they finished, part of it was already stale.

Goal

One dashboard people trust enough that they stop keeping their own version in a spreadsheet.

Users & Stakeholders

  • Business unit leads
  • Record managers
  • SharePoint owners
  • Governance/compliance partners

Solution

Designed and delivered a Power BI dashboard that pulls together:

Site Collection URLs

Every site collection in one list, so nobody's hunting through five different exports to find one.

Record Manager & Business Unit

Each library tied to the record manager and business unit actually responsible for it, so "who owns this" has one answer instead of three guesses.

Library Name

A full inventory of libraries — not just the ones someone remembered to report.

Library Size

Storage size per library, so capacity planning stops running on last quarter's numbers.

Item Counts per Library

Item counts that flag which libraries are growing fast enough to need a second look.

Outcomes

30% Reduction

Cut manual reporting effort by 30% — time that used to go into compiling numbers now goes into using them.

Near Real-Time Monitoring

Business units can check library status any time instead of waiting on the next manual report.

50% Risk Reduction

Capacity risks and non-compliant libraries dropped 50% once people could see problems forming instead of finding out after the fact.

Tech & Practices

Power BI SharePoint Online Data Modeling Stakeholder Discovery Backlog Creation Iterative Delivery Governance Alignment

Approach

  • Discovery: Talked to business units first to find out which numbers they actually needed, not which ones were easiest to pull.
  • Data Integration: Pulled the scattered sources into one Power BI model instead of one more spreadsheet.
  • Iterative Delivery: Released it in stages and let feedback from actual use shape what came next.
  • Governance Alignment: Checked every metric against our data policies before it shipped, not after.

Key Takeaways

The interesting part of this project wasn't the dashboard itself — it was watching teams change how they worked once they could see their own data. Before, capacity and compliance problems showed up as surprises. After, business units started catching them early because checking the dashboard became part of their routine. I released it in stages and let the feedback steer what came next, which kept people invested in it rather than treating it as another report pushed on them.
